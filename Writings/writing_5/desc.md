## Writing 5 - Design Document

The goal of this assignment is to communicate possible technical designs to the CSCI 3081 instructor team.  Each project has unique technical challenges to overcome and it is important to consider several alternatives before making decisions.  Your writing will help the instructor team make key design decisions about the Gopher GIS infrastructure and your team's unique extension.

### Sprint 1 Task

This writing assignment is tightly coupled to the tasks for Sprint 2:

- Each team member in your project group is assigned one feature to design (each individual team member should design a separate unique feature for your extension).
- Each individual should come up with at least two possible technical designs for the feature (object oriented design, architecture (APIs or libraries integration), database design (tables / relationships), etc...).
- Each team member needs to write about the feature design below.

### Writing Task

Please submit a document that details the following:

- Introduction
  - Describe the unique feature you are designing and how it fits into your team's project extension.
  - What technical challenge or problem does your feature help to solve?
- Design 1
  - Provide key details here about your first design.
  - Provide a diagram of your design (UML Class Diagram, DB tables and relationships, architecture diagram, etc...)
  - How does the approach solve the problem or implement the feature.  How would you implement the feature using this design?
- Design 2
  - Provide key details here about your second design.
  - Provide a diagram of your design (UML Class Diagram, DB tables and relationships, architecture diagram, etc...)
  - How does the approach solve the problem or implement the feature.  How would you implement the feature using this design?
- Discussion
  - What did you learn with respect to the two designs?
  - Compare and contrast the designs?  Explain when you would choose each one over the other one.
- Conclusion
  - What do you recommend?
  - What else should we look into?

### Writing Notes:

- **Key Criteria:** Will the instructors be able to use your writing to help make key decisions about Gopher GIS?
  - Reader - The reader are the CSCI 3081 instructors (Instructor + TAs).
  - Context - You can assume we know about Gopher GIS and the Gopher GIS base prototype code.  Do not assume we know the technical details or background of your unique prototype.
- **Quality Writing:**
  - Be clear and concise.  **Keep your writing short, but useful.**  In other words, accomplish the **key criteria** above in as few words as possible.
  - Cite your sources.
  - Provide context and structure.
  - Use good grammar and complete sentences.
  - Make logical arguments for your technical recommendations.
  - Write in paragraph form like a paper.  Do not write in a list format, however, you may include lists in your writing.
