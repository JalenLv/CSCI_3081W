## Writing 4 - Research and Prototype

The goal of this assignment is to communicate possible technical solutions and proof of concepts to the CSCI 3081 instructor team.  Each project has unique technical challenges to overcome and it is important to consider several alternatives before making decisions.  Your writing will help the instructor team make key design decisions about the Gopher GIS infrastructure and your team's unique extension.

### Sprint 1 Task

This writing assignment is tightly coupled to the tasks for Sprint 1:

- Each team member in your project group is assigned either 1 research task or 1 prototype task.
- Each individual should complete that task.
- Each individual should write about that task.

### Writing Task

Please submit a document that details the following:

- Introduction
  - Describe the research or prototype task you accomplished.
  - What technical challenge does the research or prototype help to solve?
- Approach
  - Provide key details here about your research or prototype.
  - How does the approach solve the problem and/or what did you do?
  - Share links to code and/or provide figures.
- Discussion
  - What did you learn in the research?
  - Did the prototype work?
  - Are there alternatives worth considering?  What are they and how are they different?
- Conclusion
  - What do you recommend we do based on your analysis?
  - You do not have agree with the utility of the approach.  It may be reasonable to say, "We shouldn't do this."
  - What else should we look into?

### Writing Notes:

- **Key Criteria:** Will the instructors be able to use your writing to help make key decisions about Gopher GIS?
  - Reader - The reader are the CSCI 3081 instructors (Instructor + TAs).
  - Context - You can assume we know about Gopher GIS and the Gopher GIS base prototype code.  Do not assume we know the technical details or background of your unique prototype.
- **Quality Writing:**
  - Be clear and concise.  **Keep your writing short, but useful.**  In other words, accomplish the **key criteria** above in as few words as possible.
  - Cite your sources.
  - Provide context and structure.
  - Use good grammar and complete sentences.
  - Make logical arguments for your technical recommendations.
  - Write in paragraph form like a paper.  Do not write in a list format, however, you may include lists in your writing.
